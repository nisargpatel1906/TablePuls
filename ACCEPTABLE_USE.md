# TablePulse Acceptable Use Statement (Non-Binding)

TablePulse is an Apache-2.0 open-source restaurant floor-operations platform designed to improve table readiness, guest wait-time transparency, and floor-staffing efficiency without compromising patron or employee dignity.

## Prohibited Uses (Architectural & Policy Guardrails)
TablePulse explicitly excludes and must not be modified or deployed to perform:
1. **Facial Recognition or Biometric Identification**: Identifying, tracking, or profiling individuals across visits using face embeddings, gait signatures, or re-identification vectors.
2. **Audio Surveillance**: Capturing, storing, or transcribing spoken conversations. Audio tracks are stripped at MediaMTX ingest.
3. **Demographic Inference**: Inferring age, gender, ethnicity, emotion, or health attributes.
4. **Individual Employee Disciplinary Scoring**: Measuring or ranking named staff members for punitive performance management.
5. **Covert Surveillance**: Deploying CCTV analytics without conspicuous, lawful notice to staff and patrons (`docs/templates/` provides starter EN/HI signage templates; operators must consult local legal counsel).
